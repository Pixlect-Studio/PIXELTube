# Community Guidelines

*Last Updated: October 25, 2026*

These are the current Community Guidelines for PIXELTube. All users are expected to read, understand, and abide by them.

1. **No sexually explicit or inappropriate content.** Content of a sexual nature or otherwise unsuitable for a general audience is strictly prohibited.

2. **No violent content.** This includes, but is not limited to, depictions of internal organs, extreme blood and gore, and terrorism.

3. **No hateful or discriminatory abuse.** Do not attack or demean people based on race, ethnicity, religion, or national origin. Apply this rule consistently to all people and viewpoints.

4. **No targeted harassment or threats.** Do not threaten or harass people based on their nationality, political beliefs, or other personal characteristics.

5. **No copyrighted content.** Users may not upload or share material that infringes on the intellectual property rights of others.

6. **Consequences for violations.** Users found to have violated these guidelines will face the following actions:
   - Termination of their PIXLECT account
   - Deletion of their channel
   - Removal of their videos

**PIXELTube is not an emergency service. Do not use it as one.**
