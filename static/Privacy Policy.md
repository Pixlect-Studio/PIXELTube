# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: October 15, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how PIXELTube ("we," "us," or "our") processes information when you use the demo. PIXLECT provides sign-in. Video files and application records are stored using Bunny Storage configured by the operator.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **PIXLECT profile information.** PIXELTube receives the PIXLECT user ID, username, and profile icon through its sign-in integration. PIXELTube does not receive or store your PIXLECT password. The demo stores the user ID, username, and channel name in Bunny Storage so account names, creator details, and social features can persist across browsers.
- **Content You Upload.** Videos, comments, and other material you post to PIXELTube.
- **Usage and social information.** Video view counts, watch history, likes, dislikes, comments, subscriptions, and notifications are stored in Bunny Storage. This demo's server accepts account IDs supplied by the browser and does not verify them with PIXLECT; do not rely on these records for secure or confidential account features.
- **Device and Technical Information.** IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
- **Preferences.** Theme, accent color, display name, and channel name are stored in the browser. The channel and display names are also sent with video uploads and may be stored with account and video metadata in Bunny Storage.
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies.** PIXLECT and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy practices.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain PIXELTube, including persistent video and social features
- Personalize your experience and recommend content
- Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
- Respond to inquiries and provide support
- Improve and develop the platform
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Children's Privacy

PIXELTube is intended for users aged 8 and older.

- Users aged 8 or older but under 13 must have parental consent before using PIXELTube.
- Users aged 13 or older may use PIXELTube without parental consent.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that information promptly. Parents or guardians may contact us to review or request deletion of their child's information.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is stored with the Bunny Storage account configured by the operator and may be processed by PIXLECT or other providers needed to operate the platform. We may also share information:

- **With service providers** who help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting providers), bound by confidentiality obligations
- **When required by law**, such as in response to a valid legal request
- **To protect rights and safety**, including enforcing our Terms and Community Guidelines
- **In connection with a business transfer**, such as a merger or acquisition

## 5. Content You Upload

Videos and other content you upload may be visible to other users and the public. You are responsible for the content you share and for any personal information you include in it. As stated in our Terms of Service, you are solely responsible for any damage caused to PIXELTube as a result of your content.

## 6. Data Retention

Video files, metadata, user profiles, and social records are stored in Bunny Storage until the operator removes them. The demo does not currently provide a complete account deletion or retention-control workflow. Browser preferences remain in local storage until cleared.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
